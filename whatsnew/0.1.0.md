# 0.1.0

The notes Gzyms shows once after an update to this version. Only the "- " lines are shown.

- After you unlock the Mac, the island says how many notifications came while you were away. Click it to see them.
- Do Not Disturb follows macOS Focus. Pick which Focuses in Settings → Notifications.
- Sharing, recording or mirroring the screen keeps the island quiet. Notifications wait in their lists.
- The camera and mic pill shows while an app uses them. Click it to see which app.
- Lists can keep only the last hour, day or week. Settings → Notifications → Keep in the lists.
- When Gzyms restarts a stuck Notification Center, the island tells you. Settings → Hacks counts today's restarts.
- Report a problem now says what the Mac was doing when Notification Center got stuck.
- A locked or sleeping Mac no longer counts as a stuck Notification Center.
- Choices in Settings line up on the right.
