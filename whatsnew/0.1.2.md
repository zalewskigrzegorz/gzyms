# 0.1.2

The notes Gzyms shows once after an update to this version. Only the "- " lines are shown.

- One meeting reminded by several apps now counts as the same notification, even when each app words it its own way and reminds at its own time.
- Apps you turned notifications off for in macOS stay quiet in Gzyms too.
- Plugins start again right after the Mac wakes, so their pills no longer say "No answer" for up to an hour.
